## Hello, World! 👋

Welcome to my corner of the internet! I'm thrilled you've found your way here. This portfolio represents not just my work, but my philosophy on modern web development.

## Why I Built This

After years of manually deploying projects and dreading the "it works on my machine" moments, I decided to practice what I preach: **automation first**.

This site is:
- Built with **Astro** for blazing-fast static generation
- Styled with **TailwindCSS** for rapid development
- Deployed automatically via **GitHub Actions**
- Hosted on **Netlify** with global CDN distribution

Every time I push to `main`, the site rebuilds and deploys within minutes. No SSH, no FTP, no manual steps.
